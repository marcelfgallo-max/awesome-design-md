Peptide Calculator

Derived from the Wise design language. Wise is a converter — number in, units out — which is the same interaction shape as reconstitution dosing. Adapted here for tabular numerals, dosing-state semantics, and clinical legibility.

09 — Callouts

Every color state also uses words

Each callout opens with a bold noun, not a generic "Note:". The disclaimer is persistent and non-dismissible.

Dose in range. 10 units on a U-100 syringe, comfortably readable against the graduations.

Rounded to the nearest graduation. Calculated 10.4 units, shown as 10 — a 4% difference from the requested dose.

Dose below readable range. 1.2 units cannot be measured accurately on a U-100 syringe. Use less diluent or a lower-volume syringe.

Not medical advice. This calculator performs arithmetic only. Verify every dose against your prescriber's instructions and the product labeling before administering.
